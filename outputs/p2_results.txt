Discussion
----------
(i) The elbow rule gives k_elbow = 3, the same as the Kaiser count (lambda 1-3 = 4.6450,
2.4972, 1.4309 are above 1, while lambda 4 = 0.9426 is not), but it is well below
k_0.80 = 5, k_0.90 = 7 and k_0.95 = 10. Since R(3) = 0.6595, the elbow keeps the three
main directions but drops about a third of the total variance, so it is a compact
summary rather than an accurate reconstruction. (ii) Yes, J_test(k) is non-increasing:
for any point z, train or test, ||z - z_hat||^2 = sum over j > k of (u_j^T z)^2, so going
from k to k+1 removes the term (u_(k+1)^T z)^2, which is never negative, so each point's
error can only stay the same or drop, and so can their sum. The plot agrees: J_test falls
steadily from 8.5455 at k = 1 to 0 at k = 13 and stays slightly above J_train for every
k < 13, because U was fitted on the training data.
