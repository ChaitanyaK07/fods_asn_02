Discussion
----------
(i) V1 does no scaling, so tr(S) is just the sum of the raw feature variances, and
proline alone (s^2 = 94906.7109) makes up 99.73% of it. So r1 is at least proline's
share, which gives r1 = 0.9978 with PC1 basically pointing along proline, while V3 works
on the correlation matrix where every feature has variance 1, and gets r1 = 0.3573.
(ii) V1 keeps 99.98% of the variance in two components but gives the worst 5-NN
accuracy, 0.7222, against 0.9444 for V3 and 0.9815 for V2: its projection separates
class 0 (high proline) while classes 1 and 2 overlap. Variance captured depends on the
units the features are measured in, so a high value does not mean the components carry
class-relevant structure. (iii) I would use V3 (z-score), because PCA on the correlation
matrix does not depend on measurement units and weights every feature equally. V2 scored
slightly higher here, but the gap is only two test samples out of 54, and min-max scaling
depends on the single most extreme training values, which makes it sensitive to outliers.
